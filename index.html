Why are these called NEO candidates? This local subset was selected using perihelion distance q < 1.3 AU. That means the orbit comes within 1.3 times Earth's average distance from the Sun at its closest point. It is a catalog filter, not proof of an Earth crossing or collision risk.

Model limitations: The plotted path is calculated from the catalog's osculating Keplerian elements at epoch 2026-09-17. It is a two-body approximation, not an N-body ephemeris; it does not include planetary perturbations or predict an Earth impact. The catalog's perihelion-distance filter q < 1.3 AU is used to select near-Earth-object candidates. It does not mean these objects are on a collision course.

Data: Lowell Observatory's astorb.dat. Catalog attribution: Lowell Observatory endowment and NASA PDART grant NNX16AG52G. About the astorb database.
